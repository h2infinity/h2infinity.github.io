Professor Seddik M. Djouadi is a Professor of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, and Director of the Networked Systems Laboratory.

- Ph.D., Electrical Engineering, McGill University, 1999
- Master's degree, Electrical Engineering, École Polytechnique, University of Montreal, 1992
- Bachelor's degree, Electrical Engineering, École Nationale Polytechnique, Algiers, 1989

See his [official faculty profile](https://eecs.utk.edu/people/seddik-djouadi/) for professional service and historical awards, and the laboratory's [people page]({{ '/people/' | relative_url }}) for his biography.
