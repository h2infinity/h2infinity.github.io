      ## Seddik M. Djouadi

      Professor Djouadi leads the Networked Systems Laboratory in UT Knoxville's Min H. Kao Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science. His research interests include robust and distributed control, networked control systems, wireless-system modeling, biological processes, and model reduction and control of fluid flows.

      He received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from McGill University in 1999, a master's degree in Electrical Engineering from École Polytechnique, University of Montreal, in 1992, and a bachelor's degree from École Nationale Polytechnique, Algiers, in 1989.

      [UTK faculty profile](https://eecs.utk.edu/people/seddik-djouadi/) - [Email](mailto:mdjouadi@utk.edu)

## Faculty

### Fatima Taousser

**Teaching Assistant Professor**

Fatima Taousser received her Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics in December 2015 from the Polytechnic University of Hauts-de-France, Valenciennes, France, specializing in stability and control of switched systems on non-uniform time domains. Her background spans mathematical analysis and algebra, linear and nonlinear dynamical systems, switched systems, probability and statistics, time scales theory, and machine learning.

She was a postdoctoral associate researcher in UT Knoxville's Min H. Kao Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science and a member of CURENT from October 2016 to July 2021. From August 2018 to November 2019, she also participated in a project with Oak Ridge National Laboratory's Computational Sciences and Engineering Division.

**Research interests:** stability and control of switched systems on non-uniform time domains; networked control and communication networks; multi-agent and wide-area power systems; hybrid systems, safety verification, and control; time scales theory; and machine learning.

**Education**

- Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics, Polytechnic University of Hauts-de-France, France, 2015
- M.Sc. in Mathematics, Dynamic Systems and Control, University of Sidi Bel Abbes, Algeria, 2009
- B.Sc. in Mathematics (Probability and Statistics), University of Sidi Bel Abbes, Algeria, 1999
- Bachelor's degree in French, University of Sidi Bel Abbes, Algeria, 2009

### Uzair Sharif Mughal

### Maria Batool

**Ph.D. student, Electrical Engineering**

Maria Batool is a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, advised by Professor Seddik Djouadi. She holds a B.S. and an M.Phil. in Mathematics. Her previous research focused on zero forcing sets for graph products and their connections to minimum rank and maximum nullity. Her current research interests include distributed control of networked and spatially invariant systems, with applications to vehicle platoons and drone swarms.

### Burcum Eken

**Ph.D. student, Computer Science**

Burcum Eken is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where she also earned her M.S. in Computer Engineering. Her research interests include cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and autonomous systems, with a focus on improving their security and reliability.

She previously worked as a Security Engineer, gaining experience in network security, security information and event management (SIEM), next-generation firewalls, web application firewalls (WAF), SSL/TLS security, vulnerability management, and security infrastructure. She is also a co-founder of Gazi University's Cybersecurity Research and Development Group, where she helped promote cybersecurity awareness, technical collaboration, and student engagement.

She has received three teaching awards: the Gonzalez Family Award for Outstanding Graduate Teaching Assistant in 2021–2022 and 2023–2024, and the Excellence in Graduate Teaching Assistant Award in 2022. She holds the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) and ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity certifications.

[GitHub](https://github.com/burcumeken) · [Personal website](https://burcumeken.com)

### Umarbek Guvercin

**Ph.D. student, Electrical Engineering**

Umarbek Guvercin received his B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Bilkent University, Ankara, Turkey, graduating with high honors. He is pursuing his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering in the Min H. Kao Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. He is also a Research Assistant affiliated with Oak Ridge National Laboratory.

His research interests include decentralized and stochastic control, dynamic programming, person-by-person optimality, signal processing, and machine learning applications to power and energy systems. His current work includes recursive posterior-based dynamic programming for decentralized control problems and spectral-correlation-based signal processing for event detection.
