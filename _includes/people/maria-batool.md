Maria Batool is a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, advised by Professor Seddik Djouadi. She holds a B.S. and an M.Phil. in Mathematics. Her previous research focused on zero forcing sets for graph products and their connections to minimum rank and maximum nullity. Her current research interests include distributed control of networked and spatially invariant systems, with applications to vehicle platoons and drone swarms.
