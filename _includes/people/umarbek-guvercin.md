Umarbek Guvercin received his B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Bilkent University, Ankara, Turkey, graduating with high honors. He is pursuing his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering in the Min H. Kao Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. He is also a Research Assistant affiliated with Oak Ridge National Laboratory.

<!--more-->

His research interests include decentralized and stochastic control, dynamic programming, person-by-person optimality, signal processing, and machine learning applications to power and energy systems. His current work includes recursive posterior-based dynamic programming for decentralized control problems and spectral-correlation-based signal processing for event detection.
