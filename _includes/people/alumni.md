## Alumni

### Tumin Wu - Ph.D., 2024

Doctoral research on cluster-based model reduction for fluid flows. His [UTK dissertation](https://trace.tennessee.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/18041cfc-2794-428b-b5de-b27e50e0ce4b/content), _Cluster-Based Model Reduction with Application to Fluid Flows_, is dated December 2024. He coauthored the laboratory's 2026 Cluster-POD paper.

### Bhagyashri Telsang - Ph.D., 2022

Doctoral research on decentralized resource allocation and constrained centroidal Voronoi tessellations, advised by Seddik Djouadi. [UTK dissertation record](https://trace.tennessee.edu/handle/20.500.14382/28877). Recent collaborative work includes decentralized stochastic control and grid-interactive buildings.

### Yichen Zhang - Ph.D., 2018

Co-advised by Kevin Tomsovic and Seddik Djouadi. Research on power-system frequency control and renewable integration. His [faculty profile at UT Arlington](https://pscreenlab.uta.edu/faculty/) documents his doctorate and subsequent faculty appointment.
