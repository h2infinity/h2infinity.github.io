Burcum Eken is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where she also earned her M.S. in Computer Engineering. Her research interests include cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and autonomous systems, with a focus on improving their security and reliability.

<!--more-->

She previously worked as a Security Engineer, gaining experience in network security, security information and event management (SIEM), next-generation firewalls, web application firewalls (WAF), SSL/TLS security, vulnerability management, and security infrastructure. She is also a co-founder of Gazi University's Cybersecurity Research and Development Group, where she helped promote cybersecurity awareness, technical collaboration, and student engagement.

She has received three teaching awards: the Gonzalez Family Award for Outstanding Graduate Teaching Assistant in 2021–2022 and 2023–2024, and the Excellence in Graduate Teaching Assistant Award in 2022. She holds the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) and ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity certifications.
