Professor Djouadi leads the Networked Systems Laboratory in UT Knoxville's Min H. Kao Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science. His research interests include robust and distributed control, networked control systems, wireless-system modeling, biological processes, and model reduction and control of fluid flows.

He received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from McGill University in 1999, a master's degree in Electrical Engineering from École Polytechnique, University of Montreal, in 1992, and a bachelor's degree from École Nationale Polytechnique, Algiers, in 1989.
