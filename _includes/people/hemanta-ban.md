Hemanta Ban is a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where he also earned his M.S. in Electrical Engineering. His research interests include multi-agent systems, computational fluid dynamics, autonomous systems, integrated sensing and communications, drones, and model order reduction.

<!--more-->

Before joining the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, he worked as a student research assistant at RWTH Aachen University's E.ON Energy Research Center, Institute for Automation of Complex Power Systems (ACS), in Germany. His research focused on cybersecurity and monitoring power distribution systems.
