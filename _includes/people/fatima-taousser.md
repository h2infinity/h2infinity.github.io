Fatima Taousser received her Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics in December 2015 from the Polytechnic University of Hauts-de-France, Valenciennes, France, specializing in stability and control of switched systems on non-uniform time domains. Her background spans mathematical analysis and algebra, linear and nonlinear dynamical systems, switched systems, probability and statistics, time scales theory, and machine learning.

<!--more-->

She was a postdoctoral associate researcher in UT Knoxville's Min H. Kao Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science and a member of CURENT from October 2016 to July 2021. From August 2018 to November 2019, she also participated in a project with Oak Ridge National Laboratory's Computational Sciences and Engineering Division.

**Research interests:** stability and control of switched systems on non-uniform time domains; networked control and communication networks; multi-agent and wide-area power systems; hybrid systems, safety verification, and control; time scales theory; and machine learning.
